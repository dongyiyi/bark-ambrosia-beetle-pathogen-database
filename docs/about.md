# About the database

The Bark and Ambrosia Beetle Pathogen Database is a literature-derived global compilation of organisms reported as pathogens or parasites of bark and ambrosia beetles in the subfamily Scolytinae.

The project was developed to make more than a century of scattered insect-pathology literature easier to discover, compare, and evaluate. It emphasizes both taxonomic coverage and the strength of evidence supporting each reported organism–beetle relationship.

## Current scope

The working database contains 1,011 records drawn from primary literature published between 1914 and 2026. It currently covers 114 beetle species in 29 genera and 289 candidate pathogen or parasite taxa across fungi, bacteria, viruses, nematodes, protists, and microsporidia.

These values describe the current working database and may change during final QA/QC before v1.0.

## Interpretation

Not all records represent experimentally confirmed pathogenic interactions. Some document organisms described by the original authors as pathogens or parasites without experimental validation. Detection or isolation alone does not establish active infection, pathogenicity, or causation.

The database therefore records the reported ecological relationship separately from evidence of infection, pathogenicity, virulence, or other validation.

## Maintenance

The database is compiled and curated by Yiyi Dong. Corrections and additions may be proposed through GitHub Issues. Stable public releases will be versioned and archived.
