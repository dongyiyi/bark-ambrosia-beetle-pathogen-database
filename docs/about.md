# About the database

The Bark and Ambrosia Beetle Pathogen Database is a literature-derived global compilation of organisms reported from bark and ambrosia beetles in the subfamily Scolytinae, including pathogens, parasites, and associates as classified from the source literature.

The project was developed to make more than a century of scattered insect-pathology literature easier to discover, compare, and evaluate. It emphasizes both taxonomic coverage and the strength of evidence supporting each reported organism–beetle relationship.

## Current scope

Version 1.0 contains 1,011 records drawn from primary literature published between 1914 and 2026. It covers 114 beetle species in 29 genera and 289 reported organism taxa across fungi, bacteria, viruses, nematodes, protists, and microsporidia. The literature search for Version 1.0 was completed on 24 July 2026.

## Interpretation

Not all records represent experimentally confirmed pathogenic interactions. Some records document organisms reported as associates, symbionts, parasites, or pathogens in the source literature. Detection, isolation, or association alone does not establish active infection, pathogenicity, or causation.

The database therefore records the reported ecological relationship separately from evidence of infection, pathogenicity, virulence, transmission, or other validation.

## Maintenance

The database is compiled and curated by Yiyi Dong. Corrections and additions may be proposed through GitHub Issues. Stable public versions are archived and cited through Figshare.

## Version 1.0 archive

The canonical archived Version 1.0 dataset is available at [https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33869104](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33869104).
